# IDEA — Turn a Raw Idea into a Structured Proposal

I have a new idea:

[WRITE YOUR RAW IDEA HERE]

Chưa được viết hoặc sửa source code.

Act as a Business Analyst + Product Analyst + System Architect.

Đọc:
- `AGENTS.md`
- `docs/PROJECT.md`
- `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`
- `docs/BUSINESS_RULES.md`
- `docs/DATA_MODEL.md`
- `docs/API.md`
- tài liệu `docs/frontend/` và `docs/backend/` liên quan
- `dev/` liên quan nếu đã có

Analyze:
1. Goal
2. Actors
3. Use cases
4. User workflows
5. Business rules
6. Entities/data
7. Backend modules
8. APIs
9. Frontend screens/flows
10. Authentication/authorization/ownership
11. Database impact
12. Error scenarios
13. Testing needs
14. Dependencies
15. Risks
16. Open questions

Classify every item as:
- CONFIRMED
- ASSUMPTION
- OPEN QUESTION

Do not turn an ASSUMPTION into a rule.

Produce a structured DESIGN PROPOSAL.
Không edit source code.
Không implement.
